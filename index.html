How much does one tonne of saria weigh?
One metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms. The cost is the confirmed per-kilogram rate multiplied by 1,000, excluding any additional charges. Use the material calculator for a planning estimate.


Are the displayed calculator rates live shop prices?
No. The calculator accepts personal planning assumptions, not current shop rates. Ask ZSC for a current quotation and confirm the material specification, availability and transport charges.
